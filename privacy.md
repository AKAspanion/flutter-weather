**Beautiful Weather App Privacy Policy**

*Last Updated: 03/11/2026*

Welcome to Beautiful Weather, a mobile application developed by panditankit1995@gmail.com. This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our Beautiful Weather app.

By downloading, installing, or using Beautiful Weather, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy.

### Information We Collect

#### Location Information

Beautiful Weather requires access to your device's location to provide accurate weather forecasts for your current location. We collect this information only with your explicit consent. You can enable or disable location services at any time through your device settings.

#### Personal Information

The app has no forms or accounts, and it does not ask for your name or email address. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to send us by email when you ask for support, such as your email address and the details of your question. We handle this information with the utmost confidentiality and do not share it with third parties without your consent.

#### Advertising

Beautiful Weather shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about how you interact with ads in order to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), we ask for your consent before ads are personalised; you can change that choice at any time from "Privacy" in the app menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

#### Weather Services

To fetch the weather, the app sends your coordinates (or the city name you search for) to one of the following weather services: OpenWeatherMap, WeatherAPI.com, Tomorrow.io and MET Norway. They receive only the location and your IP address as part of the request, never your name or other personal details.

#### Home Screen Widget

The widget refreshes in the background for the place you last viewed in the app. It does not access your location in the background. The last weather result is stored only on your device.

### How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected for the following purposes:

- **Weather Forecast**: Utilize your location to provide accurate and personalized weather forecasts.
- **Communication**: Respond to your inquiries, and feedback, or provide support when needed.
- **Advertising**: Show ads through Google AdMob, as described above, to keep the app free.

### Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### Third-Party Services

Beautiful Weather uses Google AdMob to show ads and the weather services OpenWeatherMap, WeatherAPI.com, Tomorrow.io and MET Norway to fetch the weather, as described above. These third parties have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page. It is your responsibility to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
